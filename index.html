Three findings

  1. 1. Unanswered 1-stars at the top Two one-star reviews are unanswered, and they’re what a new customer reads first. Silence reads as “we don’t care.” A short, professional reply to every review — good and bad — is the fastest trust fix on this profile.
  2. 2. Unanswered pricing & insurance questions Three questions sit unanswered in Q&A, including ones about pricing and insurance. Those were ready-to-buy customers asking before calling. Unanswered, they called someone else — and every future visitor sees the silence too.
  3. 3. A dead-looking profile No Google post in 120+ days and no new photos in 8 months. Next to competitors posting jobs weekly, this profile looks closed or inactive — even if the company is booked solid. Regular photos and posts are the signal that you’re open and working.
